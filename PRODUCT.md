# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (single index.html + assets), no build step. Local delivery first; Vercel deploy only after user approval.

## Users

Men and women roughly 25–60 seeking private (out-of-pocket) dental treatment — implants, orthodontics/clear aligners, veneers ("lentes de contato dental"), whitening, smile aesthetics, prostheses, preventive care. They are evaluating whether to trust a clinic with an expensive, personal, sometimes anxiety-inducing treatment, usually on their phone.

## Product Purpose

Institutional website for ELYRA ODONTOLOGIA, a fictional premium dental clinic. Primary goal: generate evaluation bookings (form + WhatsApp). Secondary: serve as a flagship portfolio case for the agency AJ Solutions Tech, so it must read as a real, high-end, non-template project.

## Positioning

Premium but approachable: digital planning (3D intraoral scan, digital smile design, digital radiography, 3D printing) plus a humane, listening-first approach. "Odontologia feita para pessoas, não apenas para dentes." Tagline: "Precisão no cuidado. Leveza no sorriso."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Booking form fields: Nome, WhatsApp, Tratamento de interesse, Melhor período para contato → success message "Recebemos sua solicitação. Nossa equipe entrará em contato em breve." (simulated, no backend).
- Interactive before/after comparator (3 cases), testimonials slider, treatment list with hover photo, floating WhatsApp button.
- Portuguese (pt-BR) only.

## Brand Commitments

Pinned by the brief (binding): palette #F7F5F0 / #EEEAE2 / #163C35 (primary identity) / #B89565 (sparing accent) / #17201D / #68706D / #FFFFFF, footer #111816. Serif display + modern sans text (brief suggests Cormorant Garamond or DM Serif Display; Inter, Manrope or DM Sans). Editorial, asymmetric, generous whitespace; no hospital look, no medical blue, no generic cards, no heavy gradients/shadows, minimal rounding. Section order and copy as specified in the brief. Footer credit: "Desenvolvido por AJ Solutions Tech."

## Evidence on Hand

Everything is fictional and supplied by the brief: 4,9 rating / 280+ reviews, +3.800 smiles, +12 years, 8 specialists, three named dentists (CRO 00000 placeholders), address Av. República, 1250 – Centro, phone (00) 0000-0000, WhatsApp (00) 99999-9999, contato@elyraodontologia.com.br. Photos are curated stock (Unsplash/Pexels); before/after images are illustrative and carry the disclaimer. No real patients, prices or results may be claimed.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion: calm, explained, never pushy.
2. Precision shown, not claimed — technology demonstrated visually.
3. Naturalness over perfection in every image and claim.
4. Every important section offers a next step (book, WhatsApp, treatments, specialists).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable comparator and sliders, reduced-motion respected, semantic HTML.
